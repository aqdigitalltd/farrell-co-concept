How we can help

Everything you’d expect from your accountant, and a bit more.

Choose a single service or combine them into one plan. Our fees are fixed and flexible, and you can pay monthly or quarterly.

Accounts & Management Accounts

Year-end accounts and tax returns without paying for things you don’t need. If your business is growing fast, we can add regular management accounts.

  • Year-end accounts
  • Management accounts

Bookkeeping & VAT

Send us your receipts and statements and we’ll keep your records and handle your VAT returns. Or keep costs down by doing some of it yourself using our simple templates.

  • Bookkeeping
  • VAT returns
  • Software setup

Business Tax

Tax planning and advice that runs all year, not just at year end. Every new client gets a free taxation health check to see which reliefs they can claim.

  • Corporation tax
  • Tax planning
  • Free health check

Personal Tax

We complete and submit your self-assessment tax return accurately for a fixed fee. That covers the self-employed, company directors, landlords, trustees and anyone with foreign income.

  • Tax returns

Payroll

We can run your whole payroll, from payslips to statutory returns, or just advise on tricky PAYE and NI questions. Payroll is usually included free with our bookkeeping, accounts or start-up services.

  • Statutory returns

About Farrell & Co

An established local practice, not an anonymous online service.

  1. The beginning Established in Hemel Hempstead as Miller Farrell
  2. 2000 Rebranded as Farrell & Co
  3. Today Serving businesses and private clients across Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire, Buckinghamshire, London and further afield

We’ve been part of Hemel Hempstead’s business community for over 20 years. Our aim has always been the same: to give clients the highest level of service at an affordable price.

We’re a small, friendly team, so you’ll be looked after by people who know you. We reply promptly, give advice before you ask for it, and always put your best interests first. Business tax is handled by experienced staff recruited from government tax offices.

Through our network of IFAs, solicitors and financial organisations, we can be a one-stop shop for your business and personal finances.
